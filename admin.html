🧾 Recipe Upload
Client chunein → Excel template download karein (usme client ke menu aur inventory ke naam pehle se milte hain) → recipe bharke upload karein (Excel / CSV / PDF) → preview check karke ek click me poori recipe push. Client ke liye Auto Stock Deduction feature ON hona chahiye.
Columns: Dish | Type | Ingredient | Qty | Unit. Type khali = recipe; direct = Pepsi jaisa seedha bikne wala item (1 row: Dish, Ingredient, Qty per sale). Dish khali ho to upar wali dish maani jaati hai. Unit g/kg/ml/l/pcs — inventory ki unit me apne aap convert hota hai.
