2. Legal Basis for Collecting Guest Data

  • The Registration of Foreigners Act, 1939 — Mandatory Form C for all hotel guests
  • Respective State Police Act — Every state in India requires hotels to register guests and maintain records submittable to police on demand (e.g. Delhi Police Act 1978 for Delhi, UP Police Act for Uttar Pradesh, and corresponding Acts in other states)
  • State Police Standing Orders / Circulars — Guest registers (commonly called the "Sarai Register" or "Form C register") must be maintained per local police directives applicable to the hotel's location
  • CGST/SGST Act, 2017 — GST invoice generation requires guest identity
  • PMLA, 2002 — Identity verification for financial transactions above threshold
  • DPDP Act 2023 — Section 4 & 6 — Processing with Data Principal's free, specific, informed consent
Form C (Pan-India Compliance): Failure to maintain hotel guest registers is punishable under applicable state police laws and Section 188, IPC. AtithiBook enables hotels across India to fulfill this mandatory obligation digitally, as permitted under IT Act 2000 — the specific state-level Act applicable depends on where the hotel operates.
